HOW TO INSTALL STOCKBUDDY ON ANDROID (Android 8.0 or newer)
===========================================================

1. Get StockBuddy.apk onto your phone. Any of these work:
   - Email it to yourself, or upload it to Google Drive, then open it on
     the phone.
   - Connect the phone to your computer with a USB cable and copy the file
     into the phone's "Download" folder.

2. On the phone, open the file (for example from the "Files" app >
   Downloads) and tap "Install".
   - Android may say: "For your security, your phone is not allowed to
     install unknown apps from this source". Tap "Settings", turn on
     "Allow from this source", go back, and tap "Install" again.
   - Google Play Protect may warn that the app is unknown, because it
     isn't from the Play Store. Tap "More details" > "Install anyway".

3. Open StockBuddy from your app list.
   - Tap the menu button (three lines, top left) to see every page:
     Today's ideas, World markets, World stocks, Economic calendar,
     Look up a stock, Price alerts, Paper trading and more.
     Hold the handle on the right of a page name and drag it up or down to
     put the pages in the order you like.
   - Swipe a page left or right to move to the next or previous page.
   - The star at the top middle opens My watchlist.
   - The three dots (top right) have: Save report (web page, Word, CSV or
     JSON), App skin, Home-screen widget skin and Widget see-through.
   - The round arrow (top right) gets new data.
   - Menu > Practice mode lets you learn with made-up data.

4. Add the home-screen widget:
   - Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, then tap "Widgets".
   - Find StockBuddy and drag it onto your home screen.
   - Change its colours and transparency in the app:
     the three dots > "Home-screen widget skin" and "Widget see-through".
   - Tap the refresh arrow on the widget to update it. It also updates by
     itself about every 30 minutes.

To uninstall: touch and hold the StockBuddy icon, then tap "Uninstall"
(or "App info" > "Uninstall").

IMPORTANT: StockBuddy is a learning and research helper, NOT a financial
advisor. It can be wrong, and you can lose money.
